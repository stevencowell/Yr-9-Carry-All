# Carry-All source and asset mapping

The source hierarchy and allowed claims are defined in `COURSE-CONTRACT.md`.

## Authoritative project resources

- `Carry-All Working Drawing.pdf` and `assets/carry-all-working-drawing.png` — the supplied working drawing. It controls the project arrangement, written dimensions, materials and rebate-and-housing construction. It must not be scaled.
- Steve Cowell's teacher authorisation of 31 July 2026 confirms that rebate-and-housing is the assessed construction. Box-pin and dovetail remain comparative theory only.
