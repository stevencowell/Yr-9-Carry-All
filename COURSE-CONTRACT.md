## Project constraints

- The assessed product uses rebate-and-housing construction.
- The working drawing identifies a **5 mm rebate butt joint** at the corner and **5 mm housing** where explicitly shown.
- The working drawing identifies **12 mm pine** components and a **3 mm plywood** base.
- The drawing specifies a **400 mm overall length** and **178 mm handle opening**.
- Written drawing information controls manufacture. Students must not scale the drawing or invent an unlabelled dimension, quantity, material, joint, tool or method.
- Box-pin and dovetail joints are retained only as comparative knowledge. They are not the required assessed joints and must not be marked or cut on Carry-All project stock.
- Local practical methods and all corrections remain teacher-controlled through SRC-04.

## Evidence and completion rules

- Knowledge checks require an answer and feedback; written responses require a genuine attempt before model comparison.
- Folio evidence must capture student thinking through a response, caption, annotated sketch or useful project photograph as directed by the card.
- A checkbox alone is not assessable evidence.
- Browser storage is device-local working storage, not submission. Students use Print / Save PDF and the folio backup controls to retain evidence.

## Exclusions

- Box-pin or dovetail wording that presents either joint as the assessed Carry-All construction.
- Generated Card 05 and Card 06 images that depict box-pin/dovetail construction as the required project pathway.
- Invented dimensions, component schedules, tools, products, operating methods, tolerances or corrections.
- Legacy repositories, old routes and unrelated Main Page or Classroom resources.
